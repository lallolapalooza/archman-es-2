ZGGEVX calcula los valores y, opcionalmente, los vectores propios izquierdos y/o derechos para matrices GE

Propósito:

!>
!> ZGGEVX calcula para un par de matrices complejas no simétricas N-por-N
!> (A,B) los valores propios generalizados, y opcionalmente, los vectores propios
!> generalizados izquierdos y/o derechos.
!>
!> Opcionalmente, también calcula una transformación de balanceo para mejorar
!> el condicionamiento de los valores propios y vectores propios (ILO, IHI,
!> LSCALE, RSCALE, ABNRM, y BBNRM), recíprocos de los números de condición
!> para los valores propios (RCONDE), y recíprocos de los números de condición
!> para los vectores propios derechos (RCONDV).
!>
!> Un valor propio generalizado para un par de matrices (A,B) es un escalar
!> lambda o una razón alpha/beta = lambda, tal que A - lambda*B es
!> singular. Normalmente se representa como el par (alpha,beta), ya que
!> existe una interpretación razonable para beta=0, e incluso para ambos
!> siendo cero.
!>
!> El vector propio derecho v(j) correspondiente al valor propio lambda(j)
!> de (A,B) satisface
!>                  A * v(j) = lambda(j) * B * v(j) .
!> El vector propio izquierdo u(j) correspondiente al valor propio lambda(j)
!> de (A,B) satisface
!>                  u(j)**H * A  = lambda(j) * u(j)**H * B.
!> donde u(j)**H es la transpuesta conjugada de u(j).
!>
!>

Parámetros

BALANC 
!>          BALANC es CHARACTER*1
!>          Especifica la opción de balanceo a realizar:
!>          = 'N':  no escalar ni permutar diagonalmente;
!>          = 'P':  permutar solo;
!>          = 'S':  escalar solo;
!>          = 'B':  permutar y escalar ambas.
!>          Los recíprocos de los números de condición calculados serán para las
!>          matrices después de permutar y/o balancear. La permutación no
!>          cambia los números de condición (en aritmética exacta), pero
!>          el balanceo sí lo hace.
!>

JOBVL

!>          JOBVL es CHARACTER*1
!>          = 'N':  no calcular los vectores propios generalizados izquierdos;
!>          = 'V':  calcular los vectores propios generalizados izquierdos.
!>

JOBVR

!>          JOBVR es CHARACTER*1
!>          = 'N':  no calcular los vectores propios generalizados derechos;
!>          = 'V':  calcular los vectores propios generalizados derechos.
!>

SENSE

!>          SENSE es CHARACTER*1
!>          Determina qué recíprocos de números de condición se calculan.
!>          = 'N': no se calcula ninguno;
!>          = 'E': se calcula solo para los valores propios;
!>          = 'V': se calcula solo para los vectores propios;
!>          = 'B': se calcula para valores propios y vectores propios.
!>

N

!>          N es INTEGER
!>          El orden de las matrices A, B, VL y VR.  N >= 0.
!>

A

!>          A es COMPLEX*16 array, dimension (LDA, N)
!>          En la entrada, la matriz A en el par (A,B).
!>          En la salida, A ha sido sobrescrita. Si JOBVL='V' o JOBVR='V'
!>          o ambas, entonces A contiene la primera parte de la forma de Schur
!>          compleja de las versiones de entrada de A y B.
!>

LDA

!>          LDA es INTEGER
!>          La dimensión principal de A.  LDA >= max(1,N).
!>

B

!>          B es COMPLEX*16 array, dimension (LDB, N)
!>          En la entrada, la matriz B en el par (A,B).
!>          En la salida, B ha sido sobrescrita. Si JOBVL='V' o JOBVR='V'
!>          o ambas, entonces B contiene la segunda parte de la forma de Schur
!>          compleja de las versiones de entrada de A y B.
!>

LDB

!>          LDB es INTEGER
!>          La dimensión principal de B.  LDB >= max(1,N).
!>

ALPHA

!>          ALPHA es COMPLEX*16 array, dimension (N)
!>

BETA

!>          BETA es COMPLEX*16 array, dimension (N)
!>          En la salida, ALPHA(j)/BETA(j), j=1,...,N, serán los valores propios
!>          generalizados.
!>
!>          Nota: el cociente ALPHA(j)/BETA(j) puede desbordarse o subdesbordarse
!>          fácilmente, y BETA(j) puede incluso ser cero. Por lo tanto, el usuario
!>          debe evitar calcular ingenuamente la razón ALPHA/BETA.
!>          Sin embargo, ALPHA será siempre menor y usualmente comparable
!>          con la norma(A) en magnitud, y BETA siempre menor y usualmente comparable
!>          con la norma(B).
!>

VL

!>          VL es COMPLEX*16 array, dimension (LDVL,N)
!>          Si JOBVL = 'V', los vectores propios generalizados izquierdos u(j) se
!>          almacenan uno tras otro en las columnas de VL, en el mismo
!>          orden que sus valores propios.
!>          Cada vector propio se escalará para que el componente más grande
!>          tenga |parte real| + |parte imaginaria| = 1.
!>          No se referencia si JOBVL = 'N'.
!>

LDVL

!>          LDVL es INTEGER
!>          La dimensión principal de la matriz VL. LDVL >= 1, y
!>          si JOBVL = 'V', LDVL >= N.
!>

VR

!>          VR es COMPLEX*16 array, dimension (LDVR,N)
!>          Si JOBVR = 'V', los vectores propios generalizados derechos v(j) se
!>          almacenan uno tras otro en las columnas de VR, en el mismo
!>          orden que sus valores propios.
!>          Cada vector propio se escalará para que el componente más grande
!>          tenga |parte real| + |parte imaginaria| = 1.
!>          No se referencia si JOBVR = 'N'.
!>

LDVR

!>          LDVR es INTEGER
!>          La dimensión principal de la matriz VR. LDVR >= 1, y
!>          si JOBVR = 'V', LDVR >= N.
!>

ILO

!>          ILO es INTEGER
!>

IHI

!>          IHI es INTEGER
!>          ILO e IHI son valores enteros tales que en la salida
!>          A(i,j) = 0 y B(i,j) = 0 si i > j y
!>          j = 1,...,ILO-1 o i = IHI+1,...,N.
!>          Si BALANC = 'N' o 'S', ILO = 1 e IHI = N.
!>

LSCALE

NAME

zggevx - Computes the generalized eigenvalues and, optionally, the left and right eigenvectors for a pair of complex conjugate matrices (A,B) of the same size.

SYNOPSIS

SUBROUTINE ZGGEVX(
     JOBVL, JOBVR, SENSE, N, A, LDA, B, LDB, ALPHA, BETA, VL, LDVL, VR,
     LDVR, RCONDV, ABNRM, BBNRM, WORK, LWORK, RWORK, IWORK, BWORK,
     INFO )
CHARACTER * 1
               JOBVL, JOBVR, SENSE
INTEGER
               N, LDA, LDB, LDVL, LDVR, LWORK, INFO
DOUBLE PRECISION
               RCONDV(*), ABNRM, BBNRM, RWORK(*), WORK(*)
COMPLEX *16
               A( LDA, * ), B( LDB, * ), VL( LDVL, * ), VR( LDVR, * ),
               ALPHA( * ), BETA( * ), WORK( * )
INTEGER
               IWORK( * )
LOGICAL

Function/Subroutine Documentation

ZGGEVX computes for a pair of complex matrices (A,B), the generalized eigenvalues

    w and optionally the vectors VL and VR such that
    A * VL = lambda * B * VL and VR * A = lambda * VR * B

where lambda is the generalized eigenvalue. If the pair (A,B) is not epimorphic, then the algorithm may not be able to compute VL and VR.

The vectors VL and VR are normalized to have Euclidean norm 1.

This routine first balances the matrix pair (A,B) by row and column scaling to improve the accuracy of the computed eigenvalues and eigenvectors. It then uses the QZ algorithm to compute the eigenvalues and eigenvectors.

Arguments

JOBVL character*1

If JOBVL = 'N', then the left eigenvectors VL are not computed.

If JOBVL = 'V', then the left eigenvectors VL are computed.

JOBVR character*1

If JOBVR = 'N', then the right eigenvectors VR are not computed.

If JOBVR = 'V', then the right eigenvectors VR are computed.

SENSE character*1

Determines which reciprocal condition numbers are computed.

    = 'N': None computed.
    = 'E': Computed for the eigenvalues only.
    = 'V': Computed for the eigenvectors only.
    = 'B': Computed for both the eigenvalues and the eigenvectors.

N integer

The number of rows and columns in the matrices A and B. 0 <= N.

A complex*16 array, dimension (LDA,*) On entry, the upper triangle and the first diagonal element of matrix A, if the matrix is upper triangular, or the lower triangle and the first diagonal element of matrix A, if the matrix is lower triangular. Complex conjugate of the remaining elements, if any, must be provided.

On exit, A is overwritten by its upper triangular representation from the QZ transformation.

LDA integer

The leading dimension of A. LDA >= max(1,N).

B complex*16 array, dimension (LDB,*) On entry, upper triangle and first diagonal element of B, if B is upper triangular, or lower triangle and first diagonal element of B, if B is lower triangular. Complex conjugate of remaining elements, if any, must be provided.

On exit, B is overwritten by its upper triangular representation from the QZ transformation.

LDB integer

The leading dimension of B. LDB >= max(1,N).

ALPHA complex*16 array, dimension (N)

The complex generalized eigenvalues. The i-th eigenvalue is ALPHA(i)/BETA(i).

BETA complex*16 array, dimension (N)

The complex generalized eigenvalues. The i-th eigenvalue is ALPHA(i)/BETA(i).

VL complex*16 array, dimension (LDVL,*) If JOBVL = 'V', VL contains the left eigenvectors such that A*VL = lambda*B*VL. The normalized eigenvector VL(i) is stored in the i-th column of VL.

If JOBVL = 'N', VL is not used.

LDVL integer

The leading dimension of the array VL. LDVL >= N.

VR complex*16 array, dimension (LDVR,*) If JOBVR = 'V', VR contains the right eigenvectors such that A*VR = lambda*B*VR. The normalized eigenvector VR(i) is stored in the i-th column of VR.

If JOBVR = 'N', VR is not used.

LDVR integer

The leading dimension of the array VR. LDVR >= N.

RSCALE DOUBLE PRECISION array, dimension (N) Details of the permutations and scaling factors applied to the left side of A and B. If PL(j) is the index of the row interchanged with row j, and DL(j) is the scaling factor applied to row j, then

       LSCALE(j) = PL(j)  for j = 1,...,ILO-1
                 = DL(j)  for j = ILO,...,IHI
                 = PL(j)  for j = IHI+1,...,N.

The order in which the interchanges are made is N to IHI+1, then 1 to ILO-1.

ABNRM DOUBLE PRECISION

The one-norm of the balanced matrix A.

BBNRM DOUBLE PRECISION

The one-norm of the balanced matrix B.

RCONDE DOUBLE PRECISION array, dimension (N) If SENSE = 'E' or 'B', the reciprocal condition numbers of the eigenvalues, stored in consecutive elements of the array. If SENSE = 'N' or 'V', RCONDE is not referenced.

RCONDV DOUBLE PRECISION array, dimension (N) If JOB = 'V' or 'B', the estimated reciprocal condition numbers of the eigenvectors, stored in consecutive elements of the array. If the eigenvalues cannot be reordered to compute RCONDV(j), RCONDV(j) is set to 0; this can only occur when the true value would be very small anyway.

If SENSE = 'N' or 'E', RCONDV is not referenced.

WORK COMPLEX*16 array, dimension (MAX(1,LWORK)) On exit, if INFO = 0, WORK(1) returns the optimal LWORK.

LWORK INTEGER

The dimension of the array WORK. LWORK >= max(1,2*N).

If SENSE = 'E', LWORK >= max(1,4*N).

If SENSE = 'V' or 'B', LWORK >= max(1,2*N*N+2*N).

If LWORK = -1, then a workspace query is assumed; the routine only calculates the optimal size of the WORK array, returns this value as the first entry of the WORK array, and no error message related to LWORK is issued by XERBLA.

RWORK DOUBLE PRECISION array, dimension (lrwork) lrwork must be at least max(1,6*N) if BALANC = 'S' or 'B', and at least max(1,2*N) otherwise.

Real workspace.

IWORK INTEGER array, dimension (N+2) If SENSE = 'E', IWORK is not referenced.

BWORK LOGICAL array, dimension (N) If SENSE = 'N', BWORK is not referenced.

INFO INTEGER

= 0: successful exit

< 0: if INFO = -i, the i-th argument had an illegal value.

= 1,...,N:

The QZ iteration failed. No eigenvectors have been calculated, but ALPHA(j) and BETA(j) should be correct for j=INFO+1,...,N.

> N: =N+1: other than QZ iteration failed in ZHGEQZ. =N+2: error return from ZTGEVC.

Further Details:

Balancing a matrix pair (A,B) includes, first, permuting rows and
columns to isolate eigenvalues, second, applying diagonal similarity
transformation to the rows and columns to make the rows and columns
as close in norm as possible. The computed reciprocal condition
numbers correspond to the balanced matrix. Permuting rows and columns
will not change the condition numbers (in exact arithmetic) but
diagonal scaling will. For further explanation of balancing, see
section 4.11.1.2 of LAPACK Users' Guide.

An approximate error bound on the chordal distance between the i-th
computed generalized eigenvalue w and the corresponding exact
eigenvalue lambda is

     chord(w, lambda) <= EPS * norm(ABNRM, BBNRM) / RCONDE(I)

An approximate error bound for the angle between the i-th computed
eigenvector VL(i) or VR(i) is given by

     EPS * norm(ABNRM, BBNRM) / DIF(i).

For further explanation of the reciprocal condition numbers RCONDE
and RCONDV, see section 4.11 of LAPACK User's Guide.

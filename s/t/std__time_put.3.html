Descripción Detallada

template<typename _CharT, typename _OutIter>

class std::time_put< _CharT, _OutIter >"Plantilla de clase principal time_put.

Este facet encapsula el código para formatear y mostrar fechas y horas de acuerdo con los formatos utilizados por strftime().

La plantilla time_put utiliza funciones virtuales protegidas para proporcionar los resultados reales. Los accesores públicos reenvían la llamada a las funciones virtuales. Estas funciones virtuales son ganchos para que los desarrolladores implementen el comportamiento que requieren del facet time_put.

NAME

std::time_put — Time formatting facet.

SYNOPSIS

#include <locale>

template<typename _CharT, typename _OutIter >
class std::time_put : public std::locale::facet;

Detailed Description

This is a locale facet that defines the virtual functions that are required to format time.

The `time_put` facet converts a `tm` structure to a character sequence. The conversions are specified by the format specifiers in the format string. The format specifiers are identical to those in strftime(3).

The template parameters are:

_CharT
The character type used by the locale.
_OutIter
An iterator type used for output.

The following table describes the format specifiers:

%a
Locale's abbreviated weekday name.
%A
Locale's full weekday name.
%b
Locale's abbreviated month name.
%B
Locale's full month name.
%c
Locale's appropriate date and time representation.
%C
Locale's century number (the year divided by 100 and truncated).
%d
Day of the month (01-31).
%D
Equal to %m/%d/%y.
%e
Day of the month, a single digit is preceded by a space ( 1-31).
%F
Equal to %Y-%m-%d.
%g
The last two digits of the year of the ISO week-numbering year (see %G).
%G
The year of the ISO week-numbering year (where weeks begin on Monday). The year of the week number is defined from Monday to Sunday of the week. The %G year is consequently the same as %g year, except that the four-digit year will be one greater than the two-digit year if the year starts on Monday and Sunday is in the previous year.
%h
Equal to %b.
%H
The hour (24-hour clock) (00-23).
%I
The hour (12-hour clock) (01-12).
%j
Day of the year (001-366).
%m
Month of the year, numeric (01-12).
%M
Minute of the hour (00-59).
%n
A newline character.
%p
Locale's equivalent of either AM or PM; blank if not known.
%r
Locale's representation of the time in 12-hour AD/PM format (e.g., 04:45:30 PM).
%R
Equal to %H:%M.
%S
Second of the minute (00-60) (60 only takes special meaning on leap seconds).
%t
A horizontal-tab character.
%T
Equal to %H:%M:%S.
%u
The day of the week (1-7); 1 is Monday.
%U
The week number of the year (Sunday as the first day of the week) (00-53).
%V
The ISO 8601 week number of year (01-53).
%w
The day of the week (0-6); 0 is Sunday.
%W
The week number of the year (Monday as the first day of the week) (00-53).
%x
Locale's appropriate date representation.
%X
Locale's appropriate time representation.
%y
Day of the year (00-99).
%Y
Year.
%z
The offset from UTC in the format +HHMM or -HHMM (empty string if the object is not a local time object).
%Z
The time zone name (empty string if the name cannot be determined).
%%
A literal '%' character.

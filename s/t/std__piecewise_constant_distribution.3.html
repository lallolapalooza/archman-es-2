template<typename _RealType = double>

class std::piecewise_constant_distribution< _RealType >"Una distribución de números aleatorios de constante por partes.

Esta distribución produce números aleatorios $ x, b_0 d sobre cada subintervalo $ [b_i, b_{i+1}) $ de acuerdo con la función de masa de probabilidad p(x | b_0, ..., b_n, ho_0, ..., ho_{n-1})
= ho_i dot ac{b_{i+1} - x}{b_{i+1} - b_i}
+ ho_{i+1} dot ac{ x - b_i}{b_{i+1} - b_i} ] para $ b_i

Description

The std::piecewise_constant_distribution class template described in this document produces random integers sampled from a discrete probability distribution. The distribution is specified by a sequence of pairs of numbers, where the first number in each pair is the value of a possible outcome, and the second number is the relative weight of that outcome.

The weights are interpreted as probabilities, and they do not need to sum to 1. The distribution normalizes them internally. If the weights do not sum to a positive value, the behavior is undefined.

Constructors are provided to initialize the distribution from a sequence of weights, optionally combined with a sequence of boundary values that define the intervals over which the distribution is constant.

Description

The Fisher F-distribution is a continuous probability distribution that arises in statistics, particularly in the analysis of variance. It describes the ratio of two independent chi-squared distributions, each divided by their respective degrees of freedom.

The probability density function (PDF) of a Fisher F-distribution with parameters m and n (degrees of freedom) is given by:

f(x; m, n) = (Γ((m+n)/2) / (Γ(m/2)Γ(n/2))) * (m/n)^(m/2) * x^((m/2)-1) / (1 + (m/n)x)^((m+n)/2)

where:

  • x is the random variable (x > 0)
  • m is the numerator degrees of freedom (m > 0)
  • n is the denominator degrees of freedom (n > 0)
  • Γ is the gamma function

The std::fisher_f_distribution class generates random numbers according to this distribution.

NAME

std::lognormal_distribution - A log-normal distribution.

SYNOPSIS

        template<typename _RealType = double>
        class std::lognormal_distribution;

DESCRIPTION

A log-normal distribution produces random numbers with a logarithm that is normally distributed. The probability density function (PDF) is defined as:

                f(x; m, s) = (1 / (x * s * sqrt(2 * pi))) * exp(- (ln(x) - m)^2 / (2 * s^2))

where:

  • m is the mean of the underlying normal distribution.

  • s is the standard deviation of the underlying normal distribution.

  • x is the value of the random number, and must be greater than 0.

The distribution has two parameters: mean and sigma.

Detailed Description

Normal distribution. A normal distribution (also known as Gaussian distribution) produces random numbers according to a normal distribution probability density function.

The probability density function (PDF) of a normal distribution is:

  PDF(x; $\mu$, $\sigma$) = $\frac{1}{\sigma \sqrt{2\pi}} e^{-\frac{1}{2}(\frac{x-\mu}{\sigma})^2}$

where $\mu$ is the mean and $\sigma$ is the standard deviation.

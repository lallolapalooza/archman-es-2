DESCRIPTION

These routines are part of the blocked Level 3 Basic Linear Algebra Subprograms (BLAS). They are designed for high performance on modern architectures, using blocking to maximize data reuse.

The routines perform matrix operations such as matrix-matrix multiplication and their checks.

subrutina ctpttf (carácter transr, carácter uplo, entero n, complejo, dimensión( 0: * ) ap, complejo, dimensión( 0: * ) arf, entero info)

CTPTTF copia una matriz triangular del formato empaquetado estándar (TP) al formato empaquetado rectangular completo (TF).

Propósito:

!>
!> CTPTTF copia una matriz triangular A del formato empaquetado estándar (TP)
!> al formato empaquetado rectangular completo (TF).
!>

Parámetros

TRANSR 
!>          TRANSR es CHARACTER*1
!>          = 'N':  Se desea ARF en formato Normal;
!>          = 'C':  Se desea ARF en formato Transpuesta Conjugada.
!>

UPLO

!>          UPLO es CHARACTER*1
!>          = 'U':  A es triangular superior;
!>          = 'L':  A es triangular inferior.
!>

N

!>          N es INTEGER
!>          El orden de la matriz A.  N >= 0.
!>

AP

!>          AP es un array COMPLEX, dimensión ( N*(N+1)/2 ),
!>          En la entrada, la matriz triangular superior o inferior A, empaquetada
!>          por columnas en un array lineal. La j-ésima columna de A se almacena
!>          en el array AP de la siguiente manera:
!>          si UPLO = 'U', AP(i + (j-1)*j/2) = A(i,j) para 1<=i<=j;
!>          si UPLO = 'L', AP(i + (j-1)*(2n-j)/2) = A(i,j) para j<=i<=n.
!>

ARF

!>          ARF es un array COMPLEX, dimensión ( N*(N+1)/2 ),
!>          En la salida, la matriz triangular superior o inferior A almacenada en
!>          formato RFP. Para una discusión más detallada, véase Notas a continuación.
!>

INFO

!>          INFO es INTEGER
!>          = 0:  salida exitosa
!>          < 0:  si INFO = -i, el i-ésimo argumento tenía un valor ilegal
!>

Autor

Univ. de Tennessee

Univ. de California Berkeley

Univ. de Colorado Denver

NAG Ltd.

subroutine DTPTTF


This subroutine copies a triangular matrix from the standard packed format (TP) to the rectangular full packed format (TF).





SUBROUTINE DTPTTF(TRANSR, UPLO, N, AP, ARF, INFO)

  CHARACTER(1)   TRANSR, UPLO
  INTEGER        N, INFO
  DOUBLE PRECISION AP( * ), ARF( * )







Purpose




  DTPTTF copies a triangular matrix A from standard packed format (TP)
  to rectangular full packed format (TF).






Parameters




TRANSR


  TRANSR is CHARACTER*1
  = 'N':  ARF in Normal format is wanted;
  = 'T':  ARF in Transpose format is wanted.



UPLO


  UPLO is CHARACTER*1
  = 'U':  A is upper triangular;
  = 'L':  A is lower triangular.



N


  N is INTEGER
  The order of the matrix A.  N >= 0.



AP


  AP is DOUBLE PRECISION array, dimension ( N*(N+1)/2 ),
  On entry, the upper or lower triangular matrix A, packed
  columnwise in a linear array. The j-th column of A is stored
  in the array AP as follows:
  if UPLO = 'U', AP(i + (j-1)*j/2) = A(i,j) for 1<=i<=j;
  if UPLO = 'L', AP(i + (j-1)*(2n-j)/2) = A(i,j) for j<=i<=n.



ARF


  ARF is DOUBLE PRECISION array, dimension ( N*(N+1)/2 ),
  On exit, the upper or lower triangular matrix A stored in
  RFP format. For a further discussion see Notes below.



INFO


  INFO is INTEGER
  = 0:  successful exit
  < 0:  if INFO = -i, the i-th argument had an illegal value






Author




Univ. of Tennessee


Univ. of California Berkeley


Univ. of Colorado Denver


NAG Ltd.





Further Details:




  We first consider Rectangular Full Packed (RFP) Format when N is
  even. We give an example where N = 6.

      AP is Upper             AP is Lower

   00 01 02 03 04 05       00
      11 12 13 14 15       10 11
         22 23 24 25       20 21 22
            33 34 35       30 31 32 33
               44 45       40 41 42 43 44
                  55       50 51 52 53 54 55


  Let TRANSR = 'N'. RFP holds AP as follows:
  For UPLO = 'U' the upper trapezoid A(0:5,0:2) consists of the last
  three columns of AP upper. The lower triangle A(4:6,0:2) consists of
  the transpose of the first three columns of AP upper.
  For UPLO = 'L' the lower trapezoid A(1:6,0:2) consists of the first
  three columns of AP lower. The upper triangle A(0:2,0:2) consists of
  the transpose of the last three columns of AP lower.
  This covers the case N even and TRANSR = 'N'.

        RFP A                   RFP A

       03 04 05                33 43 53
       13 14 15                00 44 54
       23 24 25                10 11 55
       33 34 35                20 21 22
       00 44 45                30 31 32
       01 11 55                40 41 42
       02 12 22                50 51 52

  Now let TRANSR = 'T'. RFP A in both UPLO cases is just the
  transpose of RFP A above. One therefore gets:


            RFP A                   RFP A

      03 13 23 33 00 01 02    33 00 10 20 30 40 50
      04 14 24 34 44 11 12    43 44 11 21 31 41 51
      05 15 25 35 45 55 22    53 54 55 22 32 42 52


  We then consider Rectangular Full Packed (RFP) Format when N is
  odd. We give an example where N = 5.

     AP is Upper                 AP is Lower

   00 01 02 03 04              00
      11 12 13 14              10 11
         22 23 24              20 21 22
            33 34              30 31 32 33
               44              40 41 42 43 44


  Let TRANSR = 'N'. RFP holds AP as follows:
  For UPLO = 'U' the upper trapezoid A(0:4,0:2) consists of the last
  three columns of AP upper. The lower triangle A(3:4,0:1) consists of
  the transpose of the first two columns of AP upper.
  For UPLO = 'L' the lower trapezoid A(0:4,0:2) consists of the first
  three columns of AP lower. The upper triangle A(0:1,1:2) consists of
  the transpose of the last two columns of AP lower.
  This covers the case N odd and TRANSR = 'N'.

        RFP A                   RFP A

       02 03 04                00 33 43
       12 13 14                10 11 44
       22 23 24                20 21 22
       00 33 34                30 31 32
       01 11 44                40 41 42

  Now let TRANSR = 'T'. RFP A in both UPLO cases is just the
  transpose of RFP A above. One therefore gets:

           RFP A                   RFP A

     02 12 22 00 01             00 10 20 30 40 50
     03 13 23 33 11             33 11 21 31 41 51
     04 14 24 34 44             43 44 22 32 42 52

LAPACK documentation - tpttf.3

NAME

TPTTF — TPTTF

SYNOPSIS

  SUBROUTINE TPTTF( UPLO, N, AP, ARF, INFO )
  
      CHARACTER * 1               UPLO
      INTEGER                     N
      COMPLEX*16                AP( * )
      COMPLEX*16                ARF( * )
      INTEGER                     INFO

Description

TPTTF converts a triangular band matrix from standard packed format storage to the rectangular full packed (RFP) format storage.

Parameters

UPLO

CHARACTER*1. On entry, UPLO specifies whether the matrix A is upper or lower triangular as follows:

UPLO = 'U' specifies the matrix A to be upper triangular.

UPLO = 'L' specifies the matrix A to be lower triangular.

N

INTEGER. On entry, N specifies the order of the triangular matrix A. N >= 0.

AP

COMPLEX*16 array, dimension (N*(N+1)/2)

On entry, the upper or lower triangular matrix A, packed columnwise in a linear array. The j-th column of A is stored in the array AP as follows:

if UPLO = 'U', AP(i + (j-1)*j/2) = A(i,j) for 1<=i<=j;

if UPLO = 'L', AP(i + (j-1)*(2n-j)/2) = A(i,j) for j<=i<=n.

ARF

ARF is COMPLEX*16 array, dimension ( N*(N+1)/2 ),

On exit, the upper or lower triangular matrix A stored in RFP format. For a further discussion see Notes below.

INFO

INFO is INTEGER

= 0: successful exit

< 0: if INFO = -i, the i-th argument had an illegal value

DESCRIPTION

CTGSEN reorders the generalized Schur decomposition of a complex matrix pair (A, B) (in terms of an orthonormal equivalence transformation Q**H * (A, B) * Z), so that a selected cluster of eigenvalues appears in the leading diagonal blocks of the upper triangular matrices A and B. The leading columns of Q and Z form orthonormal bases of the corresponding left and right eigenspaces (deflating subspaces). (A, B) must be in generalized Schur canonical form (as returned by CGES), i.e. A and B are upper triangular.

CTGSEN also computes the generalized eigenvalues

lambda(j) = ALPHA(j) / BETA(j)

of the reordered matrix pair (A, B).

Optionally, CTGSEN computes the estimates of reciprocal condition numbers for eigenvalues and eigenspaces. These are Difu[(A11,B11), (A22,B22)] and Difl[(A11,B11), (A22,B22)], i.e. the separation(s) between the matrix pairs (A11, B11) and (A22,B22) that correspond to the selected cluster and the eigenvalues outside the cluster, resp., and norms of projectors onto left and right eigenspaces w.r.t. the selected cluster in the (1,1)-block.

The matrix pairs (A, B) are assumed to be in the generalized Schur canonical form, i.e., A is upper triangular and B is upper triangular, and B is nonsingular.

The eigenvalues are reordered so that the selected eigenvalues (as specified by SELECT) are at the top of the diagonal blocks.

IJOB determines the behavior of the routine as follows:

IJOB = 0
Eigenvalues are reordered. No condition estimates are computed.
IJOB = 1
Eigenvalues are reordered and condition estimates are computed for the selected eigenvectors.
IJOB = 2
Eigenvalues are reordered and condition estimates are computed for the selected eigenvalues.
IJOB = 3
Eigenvalues are reordered and condition estimates are computed for the selected left eigenspaces (using Difl), right eigenspaces (using Difu), and the eigenvalues (PL and PR).

FURTHER DETAILS

 CTGSEN first collects the selected eigenvalues by computing unitary
 U and W that move them to the top left corner of (A, B). In other
 words, the selected eigenvalues are the eigenvalues of (A11, B11) in

             U**H*(A, B)*W = (A11 A12) (B11 B12) n1
                             ( 0  A22),( 0  B22) n2
                               n1  n2    n1  n2
 where N = n1+n2 and U**H means the conjugate transpose of U. The first
 n1 columns of U and W span the specified pair of left and right
 eigenspaces (deflating subspaces) of (A, B).

 If (A, B) has been obtained from the generalized real Schur
 decomposition of a matrix pair (C, D) = Q*(A, B)*Z', then the
 reordered generalized Schur form of (C, D) is given by

          (C, D) = (Q*U)*(U**H *(A, B)*W)*(Z*W)**H,

 and the first n1 columns of Q*U and Z*W span the corresponding
 deflating subspaces of (C, D) (Q and Z store Q*U and Z*W, resp.).

 Note that if the selected eigenvalue is sufficiently ill-conditioned,
 then its value may differ significantly from its value before
 reordering.

 The reciprocal condition numbers of the left and right eigenspaces
 spanned by the first n1 columns of U and W (or Q*U and Z*W) may
 be returned in DIF(1:2), corresponding to Difu and Difl, resp.

 The Difu and Difl are defined as:

      Difu[(A11, B11), (A22, B22)] = sigma-min( Zu )
 and
      Difl[(A11, B11), (A22, B22)] = Difu[(A22, B22), (A11, B11)],

 where sigma-min(Zu) is the smallest singular value of the
 (2*n1*n2)-by-(2*n1*n2) matrix

      Zu = [ kron(In2, A11)  -kron(A22**H, In1) ]
           [ kron(In2, B11)  -kron(B22**H, In1) ].

 Here, Inx is the identity matrix of size nx and A22**H is the
 conjugate transpose of A22. kron(X, Y) is the Kronecker product between
 the matrices X and Y.

 When DIF(2) is small, small changes in (A, B) can cause large changes
 in the deflating subspace. An approximate (asymptotic) bound on the
 maximum angular error in the computed deflating subspaces is

      EPS * norm((A, B)) / DIF(2),

 where EPS is the machine precision.

 The reciprocal norm of the projectors on the left and right
 eigenspaces associated with (A11, B11) may be returned in PL and PR.
 They are computed as follows. First we compute L and R so that
 P*(A, B)*Q is block diagonal, where

      P = ( I -L ) n1           Q = ( I R ) n1
          ( 0  I ) n2    and        ( 0 I ) n2
            n1 n2                    n1 n2

 and (L, R) is the solution to the generalized Sylvester equation

      A11*R - L*A22 = -A12
      B11*R - L*B22 = -B12

 Then PL = (F-norm(L)**2+1)**(-1/2) and PR = (F-norm(R)**2+1)**(-1/2).
 An approximate (asymptotic) bound on the average absolute error of
 the selected eigenvalues is

      EPS * norm((A, B)) / PL.

 There are also global error bounds which valid for perturbations up
 to a certain restriction:  A lower bound (x) on the smallest
 F-norm(E,F) for which an eigenvalue of (A11, B11) may move and
 coalesce with an eigenvalue of (A22, B22) under perturbation (E,F),
 (i.e. (A + E, B + F), is

  x = min(Difu,Difl)/((1/(PL*PL)+1/(PR*PR))**(1/2)+2*max(1/PL,1/PR)).

 An approximate bound on x can be computed from DIF(1:2), PL and PR.

 If y = ( F-norm(E,F) / x) <= 1, the angles between the perturbed
 (L', R') and unperturbed (L, R) left and right deflating subspaces
 associated with the selected cluster in the (1,1)-blocks can be
 bounded as

  max-angle(L, L') <= arctan( y * PL / (1 - y * (1 - PL * PL)**(1/2))
  max-angle(R, R') <= arctan( y * PR / (1 - y * (1 - PR * PR)**(1/2))

 See LAPACK User's Guide section 4.11 or the following references
 for more information.

 Note that if the default method for computing the Frobenius-norm-
 based estimate DIF is not wanted (see CLATDF), then the parameter
 IDIFJB (see below) should be changed from 3 to 4 (routine CLATDF
 (IJOB = 2 will be used)). See CTGSYL for more details.

Description

DTGSEN reorders the generalized Schur decomposition of a pair of matrices (A, B) so that the selected eigenvalues are at the top-left corner of the matrix pair. The function also optionally updates the Schur vectors (Schur basis) and computes error bounds for the selected generalized eigenvectors and eigenvalues.

The reordered generalized Schur decomposition has the form:

            U**T*(A, B)*V = (A11 A12) (B11 B12)
                            ( 0  A22),( 0  B22)

where U and V are orthogonal matrices. The selected eigenvalues are the eigenvalues of the subproblem (A11, B11).

DTGSEN can compute:

  • Left and right Schur vectors (columns of Q and Z).

  • Reciprocal condition numbers (lower bounds) of the left and right eigenspaces (PL and PR).

  • Error estimates (upper bounds) for the selected eigenvalues and eigenvectors (DIF).

The specific options are controlled by the parameters JOBU, JOBV, and JCJOB.

Further Details

DTGSEN first collects the selected eigenvalues by computing orthogonal U and W
that move them to the top left corner of (A, B).
In other words, the selected eigenvalues are the eigenvalues of
(A11, B11) in:

            U**T*(A, B)*W = (A11 A12) (B11 B12) n1
                            ( 0  A22),( 0  B22) n2
              n1  n2    n1  n2
where N = n1+n2 and U**T means the transpose of U. The first n1 columns
of U and W span the specified pair of left and right eigenspaces
(deflating subspaces) of (A, B).

If (A, B) has been obtained from the generalized real Schur
decomposition of a matrix pair (C, D) = Q*(A, B)*Z**T, then the
reordered generalized real Schur form of (C, D) is given by

           (C, D) = (Q*U)*(U**T*(A, B)*W)*(Z*W)**T,

and the first n1 columns of Q*U and Z*W span the corresponding
deflating subspaces of (C, D) (Q and Z store Q*U and Z*W, resp.).

Note that if the selected eigenvalue is sufficiently ill-conditioned,
then its value may differ significantly from its value before
reordering.

The reciprocal condition numbers of the left and right eigenspaces
spanned by the first n1 columns of U and W (or Q*U and Z*W) may
be returned in DIF(1:2), corresponding to Difu and Difl, resp.

The Difu and Difl are defined as:

    Difu[(A11, B11), (A22, B22)] = sigma-min( Zu )
and
    Difl[(A11, B11), (A22, B22)] = Difu[(A22, B22), (A11, B11)],

where sigma-min(Zu) is the smallest singular value of the
(2*n1*n2)-by-(2*n1*n2) matrix

    Zu = [ kron(In2, A11)  -kron(A22**T, In1) ]
         [ kron(In2, B11)  -kron(B22**T, In1) ].

Here, Inx is the identity matrix of size nx and A22**T is the
transpose of A22. kron(X, Y) is the Kronecker product between
the matrices X and Y.

When DIF(2) is small, small changes in (A, B) can cause large changes
in the deflating subspace. An approximate (asymptotic) bound on the
maximum angular error in the computed deflating subspaces is

    EPS * norm((A, B)) / DIF(2),

where EPS is the machine precision.

The reciprocal norm of the projectors on the left and right
eigenspaces associated with (A11, B11) may be returned in PL and PR.
They are computed as follows. First we compute L and R so that
P*(A, B)*Q is block diagonal, where

    P = ( I -L ) n1           Q = ( I R ) n1
        ( 0  I ) n2    and        ( 0 I ) n2
          n1 n2                    n1 n2

and (L, R) is the solution to the generalized Sylvester equation

    A11*R - L*A22 = -A12
    B11*R - L*B22 = -B12

Then PL = (F-norm(L)**2+1)**(-1/2) and PR = (F-norm(R)**2+1)**(-1/2).
An approximate (asymptotic) bound on the average absolute error of
the selected eigenvalues is

    EPS * norm((A, B)) / PL.

!>
!>  ZTGSEN first collects the selected eigenvalues by computing
!>  unitary U and W that move them to the top left corner of (A, B).
!>  In other words, the selected eigenvalues are the eigenvalues of
!>  (A11, B11) in:
!>
!>              U**H*(A, B)*W = (A11 A12) (B11 B12) n1
!>                              ( 0  A22),( 0  B22) n2
!>                                n1  n2    n1  n2
!>
!>  where N = n1+n2 and U**H means the conjugate transpose of U. The first n1 columns
!>  of U and W span the specified pair of left and right eigenspaces
!>  (deflating subspaces) of (A, B).
!>
!>  If (A, B) has been obtained from the generalized real Schur
!>  decomposition of a matrix pair (C, D) = Q*(A, B)*Z**H, then the
!>  reordered generalized real Schur form of (C, D) is given by
!>
!>           (C, D) = (Q*U)*(U**H*(A, B)*W)*(Z*W)**H,
!>
!>  and the first n1 columns of Q*U and Z*W span the corresponding
!>  deflating subspaces of (C, D) (Q and Z store Q*U and Z*W, resp.).
!>
!>  Note that if the selected eigenvalue is sufficiently ill-conditioned,
!>  then its value may differ significantly from its value before
!>  reordering.
!>
!>  The reciprocal condition numbers of the left and right eigenspaces
!>  spanned by the first n1 columns of U and W (or Q*U and Z*W) may
!>  be returned in DIF(1:2), corresponding to Difu and Difl, resp.
!>
!>  The Difu and Difl are defined as:
!>
!>       Difu[(A11, B11), (A22, B22)] = sigma-min( Zu )
!>  and
!>       Difl[(A11, B11), (A22, B22)] = Difu[(A22, B22), (A11, B11)],
!>
!>  where sigma-min(Zu) is the smallest singular value of the
!>  (2*n1*n2)-by-(2*n1*n2) matrix
!>
!>       Zu = [ kron(In2, A11)  -kron(A22**H, In1) ]
!>            [ kron(In2, B11)  -kron(B22**H, In1) ].
!>
!>  Here, Inx is the identity matrix of size nx and A22**H is the
!>  conjugate transpose of A22. kron(X, Y) is the Kronecker product between
!>  the matrices X and Y.
!>
!>  When DIF(2) is small, small changes in (A, B) can cause large changes
!>  in the deflating subspace. An approximate (asymptotic) bound on the
!>  maximum angular error in the computed deflating subspaces is
!>
!>       EPS * norm((A, B)) / DIF(2),
!>
!>  where EPS is the machine precision.
!>
!>  The reciprocal norm of the projectors on the left and right
!>  eigenspaces associated with (A11, B11) may be returned in PL and PR.
!>  They are computed as follows. First we compute L and R so that
!>  P*(A, B)*Q is block diagonal, where
!>
!>       P = ( I -L ) n1           Q = ( I R ) n1
!>           ( 0  I ) n2    and        ( 0 I ) n2
!>             n1 n2                    n1 n2
!>
!>  and (L, R) is the solution to the generalized Sylvester equation
!>
!>       A11*R - L*A22 = -A12
!>       B11*R - L*B22 = -B12
!>
!>  Then PL = (F-norm(L)**2+1)**(-1/2) and PR = (F-norm(R)**2+1)**(-1/2).
!>  An approximate (asymptotic) bound on the average absolute error of
!>  the selected eigenvalues is
!>
!>       EPS * norm((A, B)) / PL.
!>
!>  There are also global error bounds which valid for perturbations up
!>  to a certain restriction:  A lower bound (x) on the smallest
!>  F-norm(E,F) for which an eigenvalue of (A11, B11) may move and
!>  coalesce with an eigenvalue of (A22, B22) under perturbation (E,F),
!>  (i.e. (A + E, B + F), is
!>
!>   x = min(Difu,Difl)/((1/(PL*PL)+1/(PR*PR))**(1/2)+2*max(1/PL,1/PR)).
!>
!>  An approximate bound on x can be computed from DIF(1:2), PL and PR.
!>
!>  If y = ( F-norm(E,F) / x) <= 1, the angles between the perturbed
!>  (L', R') and unperturbed (L, R) left and right deflating subspaces
!>  associated with the selected cluster in the (1,1)-blocks can be
!>  bounded as
!>
!>   max-angle(L, L') <= arctan( y * PL / (1 - y * (1 - PL * PL)**(1/2))
!>   max-angle(R, R') <= arctan( y * PR / (1 - y * (1 - PR * PR)**(1/2))
!>
!>  See LAPACK User's Guide section 4.11 or the following references
!>  for more information.
!>
!>  Note that if the default method for computing the Frobenius-norm-
!>  based estimate DIF is not wanted (see SLATDF), then the parameter
!>  IDIFJB (see below) should be changed from 3 to 4 (routine SLATDF
!>  (IJOB = 2 will be used)). See STGSYL for more details.
!>

!>
!>  ZTGSEN primero recopila los autovalores seleccionados calculando
!>  unitarios U y W que los mueven a la esquina superior izquierda de
!>  (A, B). En otras palabras, los autovalores seleccionados son los
!>  autovalores de (A11, B11) en
!>
!>              U**H*(A, B)*W = (A11 A12) (B11 B12) n1
!>                              ( 0  A22),( 0  B22) n2
!>                                n1  n2    n1  n2
!>
!>  donde N = n1+n2 y U**H significa la transpuesta conjugada de U. Las
!>  primeras n1 columnas de U y W abarcan el par seleccionado de
!>  subespacios propios izquierdos y derechos (subespacios deflactantes)
!>  de (A, B).
!>
!>  Si (A, B) se obtuvo de la descomposición generalizada de Schur real
!>  de un par de matrices (C, D) = Q*(A, B)*Z**H, entonces la forma
!>  generalizada de Schur reordenada de (C, D) está dada por
!>
!>           (C, D) = (Q*U)*(U**H *(A, B)*W)*(Z*W)**H,
!>
!>  y las primeras n1 columnas de Q*U y Z*W abarcan los subespacios
!>  deflactantes correspondientes de (C, D) (Q y Z almacenan Q*U y Z*W,
!>  respectivamente).
!>
!>  Tenga en cuenta que si el autovalor seleccionado está suficientemente
!>  mal condicionado, su valor puede diferir significativamente de su
!>  valor antes de la reordenación.
!>
!>  Los recíprocos de los números de condición de los subespacios
!>  propios izquierdo y derecho abarcados por las primeras n1 columnas
!>  de U y W (o Q*U y Z*W) pueden ser devueltos en DIF(1:2),
!>  correspondientes a Difu y Difl, respectivamente.
!>
!>  Difu y Difl se definen como:
!>
!>       Difu[(A11, B11), (A22, B22)] = sigma-min( Zu )
!>  y
!>       Difl[(A11, B11), (A22, B22)] = Difu[(A22, B22), (A11, B11)],
!>
!>  donde sigma-min(Zu) es el valor singular mínimo de la matriz
!>  (2*n1*n2)-por-(2*n1*n2)
!>
!>       Zu = [ kron(In2, A11)  -kron(A22**H, In1) ]
!>            [ kron(In2, B11)  -kron(B22**H, In1) ].
!>
!>  Aquí, Inx es la matriz identidad de tamaño nx y A22**H es la
!>  transpuesta conjugada de A22. kron(X, Y) es el producto de Kronecker
!>  entre las matrices X e Y.
!>
!>  Cuando DIF(2) es pequeño, pequeños cambios en (A, B) pueden causar
!>  grandes cambios en el subespacio deflactante. Una cota aproximada
!>  (asintótica) del error angular máximo en los subespacios deflactantes
!>  calculados es
!>
!>       EPS * norma((A, B)) / DIF(2),
!>
!>  donde EPS es la precisión de la máquina.
!>
!>  Los recíprocos de las normas de los proyectores sobre los subespacios
!>  propios izquierdo y derecho asociados con (A11, B11) pueden ser
!>  devueltos en PL y PR. Se calculan de la siguiente manera. Primero
!>  calculamos L y R de modo que P*(A, B)*Q sea diagonal por bloques, donde
!>
!>       P = ( I -L ) n1           Q = ( I R ) n1
!>           ( 0  I ) n2    y        ( 0 I ) n2
!>             n1 n2                    n1 n2
!>
!>  y (L, R) es la solución de la ecuación de Sylvester generalizada
!>
!>       A11*R - L*A22 = -A12
!>       B11*R - L*B22 = -B12
!>
!>  Luego PL = (norma F(L)**2+1)**(-1/2) y PR = (norma F(R)**2+1)**(-1/2).
!>  Una cota aproximada (asintótica) del error absoluto promedio de los
!>  autovalores seleccionados es
!>
!>       EPS * norma((A, B)) / PL.
!>
!>  Existen también cotas de error globales que son válidas para
!>  perturbaciones hasta cierta restricción: Una cota inferior (x) de la
!>  norma F mínima (E,F) para la cual un autovalor de (A11, B11) puede
!>  moverse y fusionarse con un autovalor de (A22, B22) bajo perturbación
!>  (E,F), (es decir, (A + E, B + F)), es
!>
!>   x = min(Difu,Difl)/((1/(PL*PL)+1/(PR*PR))**(1/2)+2*max(1/PL,1/PR)).
!>
!>  Una cota aproximada de x puede ser calculada a partir de DIF(1:2),
!>  PL y PR.
!>
!>  Si y = ( norma F(E,F) / x) <= 1, los ángulos entre los subespacios
!>  deflactantes izquierdo y derecho perturbados (L', R') y no perturbados
!>  (L, R) asociados con el clúster seleccionado en los bloques (1,1) pueden
!>  ser acotados como
!>
!>   max-angle(L, L') <= arctan( y * PL / (1 - y * (1 - PL * PL)**(1/2))
!>   max-angle(R, R') <= arctan( y * PR / (1 - y * (1 - PR * PR)**(1/2))

Detailed Description

This routine implements the algorithm described in the references for reordering the generalized Schur factorization.

The generalized Schur factorization of a regular matrix pair (A, B) is defined as:

A = Q * S * Z**H
B = Q * T * Z**H

where S and T are upper triangular matrices. The eigenvalues of the pair (A, B) are the ratios $\lambda_i = S_{ii} / T_{ii}$.

The routine enables the user to reorder the generalized Schur form of a regular matrix pair (A, B) such that a selected subset of the eigenvalues is moved to the leading diagonal block. The Schur vectors Q and Z are updated accordingly.

The routine also computes the condition numbers for the selected eigenvalues.

Function Documentation

ORDER

[CHARACTER*1] On input, specifies whether to reorder the eigenvalues.
‘S’: reorder the eigenvalues so that the selected ones are at the top-left corner.
‘N’: no reordering is performed.


SELECT

[LOGICAL] Array of dimension N. SELECT(i) is used to specify the eigenvalues to be selected. To select the eigenvalue $\lambda_i$, SELECT(i) must be set to .TRUE.; otherwise, SELECT(i) is set to .FALSE..


N

[INTEGER] The order of the matrices A and B.


A

[COMPLEX*16 or REAL*8] On input, the upper triangular matrix S from the generalized Schur decomposition. On output, A is overwritten by the reordered matrix S.


LDA

[INTEGER] Leading dimension of A.


B

[COMPLEX*16 or REAL*8] On input, the upper triangular matrix T from the generalized Schur decomposition. On output, B is overwritten by the reordered matrix T.


LDB

[INTEGER] Leading dimension of B.


Q

[COMPLEX*16 or REAL*8] On input, the unitary matrix Q from the generalized Schur decomposition. On output, Q is overwritten by the reordered matrix Q.


LDQ

[INTEGER] Leading dimension of Q.


Z

[COMPLEX*16 or REAL*8] On input, the unitary matrix Z from the generalized Schur decomposition. On output, Z is overwritten by the reordered matrix Z.


LDZ

[INTEGER] Leading dimension of Z.


M

[INTEGER] On output, the number of selected eigenvalues.


EPS1

[REAL*8] On output, the reciprocal condition number of the selected eigenvalues.


EPS2

[REAL*8] On output, the reciprocal condition number of the remaining eigenvalues.


TRYRCOND

[INTEGER] On output, indicates whether the reciprocal condition numbers were computed.
‘1’: reciprocal condition numbers were computed.
‘0’: reciprocal condition numbers were not computed.


IWORK

[INTEGER] Workspace array of dimension N.


WORK

[COMPLEX*16 or REAL*8] Workspace array of dimension LWORK.


LWORK

[INTEGER] The dimension of the array WORK. LWORK must be at least max(1, N). If LWORK = -1, then the optimal dimension of WORK is returned.


INFO

[INTEGER] On exit, INFO is an integer value indicating whether the routine has completed successfully.
‘0’: success.
‘<0’: if INFO = -i, the i-th argument had an illegal value.
‘>0’: if INFO = i, the reordering failed to complete. This may happen if the matrix pair is close to singular.

subrutina ctfttp (character transr, character uplo, integer n, complex, dimension( 0: * ) arf, complex, dimension( 0: * ) ap, integer info)

CTFTTP copia una matriz triangular A del formato full packed rectangular (TF) al formato packed estándar (TP).

Propósito:

!>
!> CTFTTP copia una matriz triangular A del formato full packed
!> rectangular (TF) al formato packed estándar (TP).
!>

Parámetros

TRANSR 
!>          TRANSR es CHARACTER*1
!>          = 'N':  ARF está en formato Normal;
!>          = 'C':  ARF está en formato Transpuesta conjugada;
!>

UPLO

!>          UPLO es CHARACTER*1
!>          = 'U':  A es triangular superior;
!>          = 'L':  A es triangular inferior.
!>

N

!>          N es INTEGER
!>          El orden de la matriz A. N >= 0.
!>

ARF

!>          ARF es un array COMPLEX, dimension ( N*(N+1)/2 ),
!>          En la entrada, la matriz triangular superior o inferior A almacenada en
!>          formato RFP. Para una mayor discusión ver Notas abajo.
!>

AP

!>          AP es un array COMPLEX, dimension ( N*(N+1)/2 ),
!>          En la salida, la matriz triangular superior o inferior A, empaquetada
!>          por columnas en un array lineal. La columna j de A se almacena
!>          en el array AP como sigue:
!>          si UPLO = 'U', AP(i + (j-1)*j/2) = A(i,j) para 1<=i<=j;
!>          si UPLO = 'L', AP(i + (j-1)*(2n-j)/2) = A(i,j) para j<=i<=n.
!>

INFO

!>          INFO es INTEGER
!>          = 0:  salida exitosa
!>          < 0:  si INFO = -i, el i-ésimo argumento tenía un valor ilegal
!>

Autor

Univ. de Tennessee

Univ. de California Berkeley

Univ. de Colorado Denver

NAG Ltd.

subroutine   dtfttp (character transr, character uplo, integer n, double precision, dimension( 0: * )   arf, double precision, dimension( 0: * ) ap, integer info)


DTFTTP copies a triangular matrix from the rectangular full
    packed format (TF) to the standard packed format (TP).


Purpose:




!>
!> DTFTTP copies a triangular matrix A from rectangular full packed
!> format (TF) to standard packed format (TP).
!> 




Parameters


TRANSR

!>          TRANSR is CHARACTER*1
!>          = 'N':  ARF is in Normal format;
!>          = 'T':  ARF is in Transpose format;
!> 


UPLO


!>          UPLO is CHARACTER*1
!>          = 'U':  A is upper triangular;
!>          = 'L':  A is lower triangular.
!> 


N


!>          N is INTEGER
!>          The order of the matrix A. N >= 0.
!> 


ARF


!>          ARF is DOUBLE PRECISION array, dimension ( N*(N+1)/2 ),
!>          On entry, the upper or lower triangular matrix A stored in
!>          RFP format. For a further discussion see Notes below.
!> 


AP


!>          AP is DOUBLE PRECISION array, dimension ( N*(N+1)/2 ),
!>          On exit, the upper or lower triangular matrix A, packed
!>          columnwise in a linear array. The j-th column of A is stored
!>          in the array AP as follows:
!>          if UPLO = 'U', AP(i + (j-1)*j/2) = A(i,j) for 1<=i<=j;
!>          if UPLO = 'L', AP(i + (j-1)*(2n-j)/2) = A(i,j) for j<=i<=n.
!> 


INFO


!>          INFO is INTEGER
!>          = 0:  successful exit
!>          < 0:  if INFO = -i, the i-th argument had an illegal value
!> 




Author

Univ. of California Berkeley


Univ. of Colorado Denver


NAG Ltd.

!>
!>  We first consider Rectangular Full Packed (RFP) Format when N is
!>  even. We give an example where N = 6.
!>
!>      AP is Upper             AP is Lower
!>
!>   00 01 02 03 04 05       00
!>      11 12 13 14 15       10 11
!>         22 23 24 25       20 21 22
!>            33 34 35       30 31 32 33
!>               44 45       40 41 42 43 44
!>                  55       50 51 52 53 54 55
!>
!>
!>  Let TRANSR = 'N'. RFP holds AP as follows:
!>  For UPLO = 'U' the upper trapezoid A(0:5,0:2) consists of the last
!>  three columns of AP upper. The lower triangle A(4:6,0:2) consists of
!>  the transpose of the first three columns of AP upper.
!>  For UPLO = 'L' the lower trapezoid A(1:6,0:2) consists of the first
!>  three columns of AP lower. The upper triangle A(0:2,0:2) consists of
!>  the transpose of the last three columns of AP lower.
!>  This covers the case N even and TRANSR = 'N'.
!>
!>         RFP A                   RFP A
!>
!>        03 04 05                33 43 53
!>        13 14 15                00 44 54
!>        23 24 25                10 11 55
!>        33 34 35                20 21 22
!>        00 44 45                30 31 32
!>        01 11 55                40 41 42
!>        02 12 22                50 51 52
!>
!>  Now let TRANSR = 'T'. RFP A in both UPLO cases is just the
!>  transpose of RFP A above. One therefore gets:
!>
!>
!>           RFP A                   RFP A
!>
!>     03 13 23 33 00 01 02    33 00 10 20 30 40 50
!>     04 14 24 34 44 11 12    43 44 11 21 31 41 51
!>     05 15 25 35 45 55 22    53 54 55 22 32 42 52
!>
!>
!>  We then consider Rectangular Full Packed (RFP) Format when N is
!>  odd. We give an example where N = 5.
!>
!>     AP is Upper                 AP is Lower
!>
!>   00 01 02 03 04              00
!>      11 12 13 14              10 11
!>         22 23 24              20 21 22
!>            33 34              30 31 32 33
!>               44              40 41 42 43 44
!>
!>
!>  Let TRANSR = 'N'. RFP holds AP as follows:
!>  For UPLO = 'U' the upper trapezoid A(0:4,0:2) consists of the last
!>  three columns of AP upper. The lower triangle A(3:4,0:1) consists of
!>  the transpose of the first two columns of AP upper.
!>  For UPLO = 'L' the lower trapezoid A(0:4,0:2) consists of the first
!>  three columns of AP lower. The upper triangle A(0:1,1:2) consists of
!>  the transpose of the last two columns of AP lower.
!>  This covers the case N odd and TRANSR = 'N'.
!>
!>         RFP A                   RFP A
!>
!>        02 03 04                00 33 43
!>        12 13 14                10 11 44
!>        22 23 24                20 21 22
!>        00 33 34                30 31 32
!>        01 11 44                40 41 42

!>           RFP A                   RFP A

SYNOPSIS


 SUBROUTINE ZTFTTP( UPLO, TRANSR, N, ARF, AP, INFO )

      CHARACTER * 1 UPLO
      CHARACTER * 1 TRANSR
      INTEGER N
      COMPLEX*16 ARF( * )
      COMPLEX*16 AP( * )
      INTEGER INFO







Detailed Description


ZTFTTP converts a triangular factor of a Hermitian matrix
    stored in the RFP format into triangular-packed format.


A Hermitian matrix H may be represented in RFP format, i.e.
    H = P * A * P^T, where P is a permutation matrix and A is a triangular
    matrix. It is sometimes more efficient to store the triangular factor A
    in the RFP format.


This subroutine converts the triangular factor A from RFP
    format to a format used by the routines which are tiled for
    performance.






 UPLO is CHARACTER*1
        On entry, UPLO specifies the nature of the triangular
        factor.
        UPLO = 'U' or 'u'   A is upper triangular.
        UPLO = 'L' or 'l'   A is lower triangular.

 TRANSR is CHARACTER*1
        On entry, TRANSR specifies the relationship between the
        triangular factor A and the matrix A in the RFP format.
        See the description of the LAYOUT parameter in Section 3.3 of
        the LAPACK User's Guide for details.
        TRANSR = 'N' or 'n'   A is the triangular factor.
        TRANSR = 'C' or 'c'   A is the transpose of the triangular
                              factor.

 N is INTEGER
        On entry, N is the order of the upper or lower triangular
        submatrix A.

 ARF is COMPLEX*16 array, dimension (N*(N+1)/2)
        On entry, ARF contains the upper or lower triangular matrix A,
        packed out-of-place.

 AP is COMPLEX*16 array, dimension (N*(N+1)/2)
        On exit, AP contains the upper or lower triangular matrix A,
        packed columnwise in a linear array. The j-th column of A is stored
        in the array AP as follows:
        if UPLO = 'U', AP(i + (j-1)*j/2) = A(i,j) for 1<=i<=j;
        if UPLO = 'L', AP(i + (j-1)*(2n-j)/2) = A(i,j) for j<=i<=n.

 INFO is INTEGER
        = 0:  successful exit
        < 0:  if INFO = -i, the i-th argument had an illegal value

  We first consider Standard Packed Format when N is even.
  We give an example where N = 6.

      AP is Upper             AP is Lower

   00 01 02 03 04 05       00
      11 12 13 14 15       10 11

  Let TRANSR = 'N'. RFP holds AP as follows:
  For UPLO = 'U' the upper trapezoid A(0:5,0:2) consists of the last
  three columns of AP upper. The lower triangle A(4:6,0:2) consists of
  conjugate-transpose of the first three columns of AP upper.
  For UPLO = 'L' the lower trapezoid A(1:6,0:2) consists of the first
  three columns of AP lower. The upper triangle A(0:2,0:2) consists of
  conjugate-transpose of the last three columns of AP lower.
  To denote conjugate we place -- above the element. This covers the
  case N even and TRANSR = 'N'.

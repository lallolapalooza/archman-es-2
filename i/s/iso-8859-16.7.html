NOMBRE

iso_8859-16 - ISO/IEC 8859-16:1999 character set

DESCRIPCIÓN

The ISO/IEC 8859-16 character encoding is an extension of the ASCII character set. It defines characters for Western European languages.

The encoding is based on the ISO 8859 standard. It includes characters from ISO 8859-1 (Latin-1) plus additional characters for languages such as Albanian, Croatian, Finnish, French, Hungarian, Irish, Italian, Polish, Romanian, Slovak, Slovenian and Welsh.

The table below shows the mapping of the characters from ISO 8859-16. The first column is the decimal value, the second is the octal value, the third is the hexadecimal value, the fourth is the character itself, and the fifth is the character name.

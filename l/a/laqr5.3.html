NAME

slaqr5, zlaqr5 - Performs a single small-bulge multi-shift QR sweep.

                 NH, WH, LDWH)

DESCRIPTION

SLAQR5 performs a single small-bulge multi-shift QR sweep for real matrices.

ZLAQR5 performs a single small-bulge multi-shift QR sweep for complex matrices.

These routines are called by SLAQR0 and ZLAQR0, respectively, to perform a single QR sweep. The purpose of the QR sweep is to introduce shifts to accelerate the convergence of the eigenvalues.

The small-bulge multi-shift QR sweep is a generalization of the standard single-shift QR algorithm. It performs a sequence of Givens rotations to introduce shifts and reduce the subdiagonal elements of the Hessenberg matrix, thus helping to isolate eigenvalues.

The routine operates on an isolated block of the Hessenberg matrix defined by the rows and columns from KTOP to KBOT. This block is assumed to be separate from the rest of the matrix, meaning that the subdiagonal elements H(KTOP, KTOP-1) and H(KBOT+1, KBOT) are zero (or KTOP=1 and KBOT=N).

The shifts are provided in the arrays SR and SI (for SLAQR5) or S (for ZLAQR5). NSHFTS specifies the number of shifts, which must be a positive even integer.

The routine can optionally accumulate the transformations into an output matrix Z, if WANTZ is set to .TRUE..

The parameter KACC22 controls how far-from-diagonal orthogonal updates are computed. It can be 0, 1, or 2. The value 2 is used to exploit the 2-by-2 structure during matrix multiplications, but this is no longer supported.

The routine uses several workspace arrays (V, U, WV, WH) to store intermediate results and facilitate the matrix-matrix multiplication operations.
